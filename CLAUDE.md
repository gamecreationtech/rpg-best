# Falling Sky

Falling Sky is a role-playing game in early pre-production. The engine has not been chosen yet.

## Conventions

- Design decisions live in `docs/design/`. Update the design doc when a decision is made.
- Do not add engine-specific scaffolding until the engine is decided and recorded in the design doc.
- Keep the README's status section current.
- Large binary assets (textures, audio, models) go through Git LFS once configured. Do not commit them raw.
- Commit messages: short imperative subject line, body explaining why when it is not obvious.
