# Falling Sky: Game Design Document

Status: foundation decided on 2026-09-27. Sections marked TBD are still open.

## 1. Overview

- **Working title:** Falling Sky
- **Genre:** Isometric action role-playing game in the tradition of Diablo 2
- **Platforms:** Web browser on phones, tablets and desktop. Installable as a
  Progressive Web App (home-screen icon, full screen, offline capable).
- **Engine:** Custom game layer on Three.js, written in TypeScript
- **Target audience:** Diablo 2 veterans and action RPG players who want a real
  loot game on their phone with no download
- **One-line pitch:** A grim gothic Diablo 2 style loot game that runs in any
  browser, looks far better than its download size suggests, and stays smooth
  with two hundred monsters on screen.

## 2. Core pillars

Every feature must serve at least one of these. If it does not, cut it.

1. **The loot hunt.** Killing monsters drops gear with random properties. Finding,
   comparing and equipping items is the heartbeat of the game.
2. **Crowd combat that stays smooth.** Big packs of monsters and big spells, at a
   steady 60 frames per second on a mid-range phone. Performance is a feature.
3. **Grim beauty from pure code.** Every model, texture, animation and sound is
   generated by code. The look is flat-shaded low-poly, sold by dramatic
   lighting, fog, particles and post-processing. Nothing may look like a
   placeholder.
4. **One thumb is enough.** Tap to move, tap to attack, tap a skill. The whole
   game is playable on a phone held in one hand.

## 4. Gameplay

### 4.1 Core loop

- **Minute to minute:** Tap to move through a dark procedurally generated area,
  fight packs of monsters with spells, pick up drops, manage health and mana.
- **Session (10 to 30 minutes):** Clear a dungeon level or two, portal back to
  town, sell junk, equip upgrades, spend skill points, go deeper.
- **Long term:** Push through acts, hunt rare and unique items, build the hero
  toward a personal playstyle through the skill trees.

### 4.2 Heroes and progression

- **First hero: the Sorcerer.** Ranged spellcaster with big visual effects.
  Chosen first because it showcases the crowd-and-spells fantasy and stresses
  the effects budget from day one. Working kit: fire (Fireball, Meteor), cold
  (Frost Nova, Ice Bolt), lightning (Chain Lightning, Nova).
- **Later heroes:** Warrior (melee, whirlwind, shouts) and Ranger (bows, traps).
- **Skills:** Three skill trees per hero, Diablo 2 style. One skill point per
  level, invested permanently. Respec is rare and costly.
- **Stats:** Strength, Dexterity, Vitality, Energy, with attribute points on
  level up. Exact formulas TBD.

### 4.3 Combat

- **Camera:** Fixed isometric camera, angle and zoom tuned for phone screens.
- **Controls:** Tap or click to move and attack. Skill bar at the bottom of the
  screen with a small number of slots. Same scheme on phone and desktop.
- **Scale:** Designed for packs. Up to two hundred monsters may be alive and
  visible at once. Monsters are threatening in numbers, not individually.
- **Difficulty:** Normal, Nightmare and Hell, unlocked in sequence. Details TBD.

### 4.4 Levels and world structure

- **Procedural generation every run,** like Diablo 2. Layout rules are
  hand-designed, the arrangement is random. Towns and boss arenas are fixed.
- Acts, each with a town, several outdoor and dungeon areas, and an act boss.
  Number of acts TBD; the vertical slice has one act with one dungeon.

### 4.5 Items

- **Rarity tiers:** Normal, Magic, Rare and Unique.
- **Random affixes:** Magic items roll one prefix or suffix, rares roll several.
  Uniques have fixed named properties.
- **Slots:** Weapon, off-hand, helmet, body armour, gloves, boots, belt, two
  rings, amulet.
- **Visible gear:** Equipped items change the hero's appearance.
- **Later:** Sets, sockets, runes, gems, charms.

### 4.6 Death

- Respawn in town with no penalty. Friendly for short phone sessions.
- Hardcore mode may be revisited later.

### 4.7 Saves

- Saved in the browser on the device. Autosave on town visits and level exits.
- Export and import a save code to move between devices.
- Cloud saves arrive with co-op, when a server exists anyway.

### 4.8 Multiplayer

- Single-player first. Simulation is kept separate from rendering so that
  server-authoritative co-op can be added later without a rewrite.

## 5. Art and audio direction

- **Rule:** All art is generated by code at runtime. No image, model, texture,
  font or audio files are committed. See the technical design for what this
  means in practice.
- **Style:** Flat-shaded low-poly with dramatic lighting. Colour and silhouette
  carry the look. Characters are built from a few rigid parts so equipment can
  swap in and out.
- **Lighting:** One moonlight or torch-coloured key light with shadows, thick
  fog, strong vignette and colour grading toward cold greys and warm torch
  orange. Spells glow.
- **Readability:** Monsters must be distinguishable by silhouette and colour at
  phone size. Player, allies, enemies and loot each have a reserved colour
  language.
- **Audio:** Synthesised with the Web Audio API. Percussive hits, low drones,
  crackling spells. Music is generative and sparse.
